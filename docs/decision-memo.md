# Decision memo

## Recommendation

Treat the synthetic results as evidence that the prespecified SEM workflow functions as intended, not as evidence about real students. For a real study, validate the instrument and measurement invariance before interpreting structural paths or group differences.

## Why measurement comes first

A structural coefficient is difficult to interpret when the latent constructs are measured poorly or differently across groups. The project therefore requires acceptable CFA fit, interpretable loadings, reliability, and group invariance before estimating the structural model.

## Reporting priorities

1. Report the measurement model before the structural paths.
2. Provide robust global-fit indices and parameter-level estimates.
3. Describe missingness and the assumptions behind FIML.
4. Report specific indirect effects with confidence intervals.
5. Avoid selecting paths solely from modification indices.
6. Separate association, mediation language, and causal claims.
7. Acknowledge unmodeled clustering and test it in a real application.

## Decision boundary

The analysis can support a theory-consistent description of how latent constructs covary in the synthetic data. It cannot determine that support causes engagement, that engagement causes confidence, or that any single path represents an intervention mechanism.
